How to Send an Email Blast Without Mailchimp (Free & No Login)

Sometimes you do not need a drag-and-drop newsletter builder, audience segmentation, or a 14-step campaign wizard. You just need to message 25 people right now before an event starts, a room changes, or a limited drop goes live.

Why Gmail BCC and Mailchimp Both Fall Short

  • Gmail BCC is manual and error-prone: Copying and pasting 30 addresses every time leads to missed members, accidental "Reply All" chaos, and daily sending blocks from your personal inbox.
  • Mailchimp is built for corporate marketing: Creating an account, verifying physical mailing addresses, formatting HTML blocks, and managing CSV imports takes 20 minutes just to send two sentences—and heavy HTML templates often land straight in Gmail's Promotions tab where nobody sees them on time.

How Tag-Based Email Blasts Work Instead

  1. Claim a tag in 5 seconds: Visit snapmail.live, pick a memorable tag like #sunday-run or #vip-drop, and lock it with a 4-digit Creator PIN.
  2. Let your crew opt themselves in: Share your direct tag link or QR code. Anyone who wants updates enters their email once—no spreadsheets, passwords, or manual list imports required.
  3. Blast straight to the Primary inbox: Type your update from your phone (with an optional flyer image or action button) and hit send. Clean, fast alerts arrive right in subscribers' Primary inboxes so they read your update immediately.

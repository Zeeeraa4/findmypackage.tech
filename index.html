Safe, legal ways to get it back

  1. 1

    Ask the carrier to retrieve it

    Recommended

    The carrier sends its own courier to collect it from where it was left. You never have to meet anyone.

  2. 2

    Request redelivery, a replacement, or a refund

    Report the order as misdelivered in your account or with the seller. Many sellers will reship or refund without the original package coming back.

  3. 3

    Report the misdelivery to the carrier

    Contact the carrier with your tracking number so it can reroute or recover the package.

Keep it safe and legal

  • Don't go looking for the package or approach anyone. Let the carrier handle retrieval.
  • Never enter private property or collect a package from someone else's doorstep or building without the carrier's or the occupant's clear OK.
  • FindMyPackage.tech doesn't connect you with the finder or arrange a handoff. That is what keeps everyone safe.
  • Keep your tracking number out of public posts and messages.
